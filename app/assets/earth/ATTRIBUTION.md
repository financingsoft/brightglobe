# Earth texture attribution

Earth Day Map, Earth Night Map, and Earth Clouds are provided by [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/).

These textures are distributed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They may be adapted and used commercially with attribution.

Changes made for BrightGlobe: images are resized to a maximum of 2048×1024; the cloud map is converted to grayscale; all images are optimized as JPEGs.
